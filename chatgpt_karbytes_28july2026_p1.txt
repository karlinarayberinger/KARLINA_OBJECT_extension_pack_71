[Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/articles/23234-hypnagogic-hallucinations), [review in *Schizophrenia Bulletin*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4988750/)

* Partially closed eyelids reduce and unevenly filter the screen’s light. Infrequent blinking can also destabilize the tear film, adding blur, halos, streaking, and colored fringes.

Free-floating colored geometry over a featureless field is more likely generated by the retina/visual cortex, with the screen acting as a blank projection surface. Recent cannabis or psychedelic use can sometimes intensify such pattern perception, but sleep-onset-only patterns do not by themselves indicate persistent perceptual disorder.

A useful informal check is to wake yourself fully, blink several times, open your eyes completely, brighten the room, and look at a detailed object. If the pattern disappears promptly, that strongly favors the sleep-onset/Ganzfeld explanation. Arrange a routine eye or medical evaluation if it starts occurring while fully alert, becomes persistent, is consistently confined to one eye, or is accompanied by headaches or other neurological symptoms. Seek urgent eye care for sudden flashes accompanied by many new floaters, lost vision, or a curtain/shadow across the visual field, which can indicate retinal detachment. [National Eye Institute](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/retinal-detachment)

What is the physical cause of the qualia I described? Why those colors and patterns in particular?

ChatGPT:

[Worked for 47s]

The best current physical account is: **self-organizing electrochemical activity in your visual system becomes perceptible because drowsiness, a nearly featureless screen, and recent psilocybin jointly weaken the dominance of ordinary retinal input.**

Why repeating geometry:

Your primary visual cortex, V1, is arranged as a map of visual space containing interconnected neurons selective for edges, orientations, spatial frequencies, and positions. When those circuits receive little structured input but remain excitable, their activity can synchronize into simple repeating modes—much like standing waves forming on a vibrating surface.

Mathematical models show that stripe-like neural activity in the flattened cortical map transforms through V1’s retinotopic geometry into the familiar “form constants”:

* Parallel activity → gratings and checkerboards

* Intersecting waves → lattices, honeycombs, and cobwebs

* Curved or rotating activity → spirals

* Radial activity → tunnels and funnels

These patterns recur across hypnagogia, migraine aura, flicker, sensory deprivation, and psychedelics because they reflect the shared physical architecture of human visual cortex—not necessarily memories or symbolic imagery. [Bressloff et al., geometric-hallucination model](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1088430/)

Why neon rainbow colors:

Color is encoded through competing neural channels rather than by a single “color detector.” Cone signals are reorganized into roughly red-versus-green, blue-versus-yellow, and light-versus-dark channels. Color and form signals are then combined throughout V1 and later visual areas. [Research on cone-opponent domains in V1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9596481/)

When you stare at gray, those opponent channels receive approximately balanced stimulation. Adaptation suppresses the stable gray signal. Consequently, small spontaneous imbalances between color-selective populations become disproportionately visible. Alternating activity among opponent populations produces highly separated complementary hues rather than muddy intermediate colors. Against dark gray, even weak internally generated chromatic signals can therefore look fluorescent or “Lisa Frank neon.”

Psilocybin strengthens the effect by activating serotonin **5-HT2A receptors** and altering gain, excitability, contextual processing, and communication within visual cortex. Blocking 5-HT2A receptors substantially suppresses psilocybin-induced visual alterations, providing direct evidence for that mechanism. [Kometer et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6618596/) Recent research also shows that psilocybin changes contextual computations in V1–V3. [*Nature Communications* study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-65150-y)

Why it appears superimposed on the screen:

The real gray screen and the internally generated pattern are processed within the same retinotopic coordinate system. Your brain therefore does not necessarily tag the weak pattern as a separate mental image; it is experienced as a translucent texture belonging to the surface you are viewing.

When you open your eyes fully and focus, retinal contrast, eye movements, attention, and wake-promoting brain activity increase. Structured bottom-up signals regain control, and the weak self-generated pattern falls below conscious threshold. Homogeneous-field research similarly suggests that reduced structured input permits internal visual noise to be treated as signal. [Ganzfeld research](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-52372-1)

This explains the likely physical machinery and why it favors those colors and geometries. It does **not** completely solve the philosophical question of why activity in those circuits *feels* like neon color—the explanatory gap concerning qualia remains unresolved. Neuroscience can currently explain why particular neural configurations reliably correspond to particular experiences, but not why electrochemical activity possesses subjective appearance at all.
